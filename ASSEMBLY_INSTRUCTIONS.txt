PureEra Website - Split Download Package

Because the complete website is about 2.7 GB, it has been split into smaller archives.

How to assemble:
1. Extract 00_PureEra_Core.zip into a new folder, for example PureEra_Website.
2. Extract EVERY recipe part ZIP into that same PureEra_Website folder.
3. When asked to merge folders, choose Merge/Yes. Do not rename folders.
4. After all parts are extracted, the site will have index.html plus recipes/... and will work as the complete codebase.

All recipe part ZIPs preserve the correct recipes/<Category>/<Recipe>/ folder paths.
