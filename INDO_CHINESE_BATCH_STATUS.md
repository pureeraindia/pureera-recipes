# Indo-Chinese Recipe Batch

Integrated 10 Indo-Chinese recipes from `INDO-CHINESE(1).zip`.

Each recipe now has:
- three PNG recipe images;
- first image used as the recipe-card preview;
- View Recipe gallery support with left/right arrows;
- one recipe ZIP containing all three images for Download Recipe.

Recipe names were normalized from the visible titles in the supplied cover images.

Five supplied Page2/PHOTO CHANGE images were matched to their dishes and used as the recipe page-2 image.
